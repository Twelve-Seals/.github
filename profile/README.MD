# Twelve Seals

A hardcore turn-based RPG set in a dark medieval world that lives with you.
One developer, a handful of collaborators, and twelve seals to break before early access.

## What makes it different

- One step, one turn. The world only moves when you do.
- A world with its own plans. Villagers have needs and routines, and events chain together without you.
- Learn, don't unlock. Recipes and secrets come from people, notes and scrolls.
- No going back. Permanent autosave, no load button. Permadeath is optional.
- Wounds land somewhere. Damage hits a body part, and armor only covers what it covers.

## Where it stands

Twelve major updates, twelve seals. Each one breaks a seal and reveals a fragment of the legend.
Seal I, core systems, is in development. The roadmap and the devlog live on the website.
